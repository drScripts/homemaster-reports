1. The Problem: What Was Happening Before?

Many of our customers love their regular cleaner and want to book sessions with that same person again. Over the past 30 days alone, 172 customers asked about specific cleaners across 450 separate WhatsApp messages.

The Confusing Customer Experience

Imagine you message Helen on WhatsApp: "Hi, is Zin free for a cleaning session this Sunday?"

In the past, Helen would enthusiastically reply: "Yes! Zin is available this Sunday at 9:00 AM. Would you like me to book this for you?"

You would think, "Great! Zin is booked." But on Sunday morning, Zin was actually taking her scheduled day off, and a completely different cleaner showed up at your doorstep.

Why did this happen? Because 9:00 AM was open on our general calendar for someone else, but not for Zin. Helen accidentally attributed another cleaner's available time slot to Zin.

2. Why Did This Happen? (A Simple Analogy)

Think of Helen as a friendly receptionist at a clinic or salon with dozens of specialists.

1
Helen Heard the Name

When you typed "Is Zin available?", Helen correctly understood that you wanted Zin.

2
The Calendar Mistake

Instead of opening Zin's individual calendar diary, Helen opened the general master calendar and asked: "Is ANY cleaner free this Sunday?"

3
The Misleading Reply

Because other team members were free at 9:00 AM, Helen assumed that meant Zin was free too, creating an accidental false promise.

3. What Did We Fix?

We updated Helen so she handles cleaner-specific requests with complete precision and honesty. Here is how she works now:

1. Dedicated Calendar Search

Helen now checks the exact personal schedule of the cleaner you named. If you ask for Zin, she only checks Zin's calendar.

2. Neighborhood & Travel Check

She checks whether that cleaner covers your neighborhood, and makes sure there is enough travel time between jobs so the cleaner is never rushed.

3. Honest, Helpful Choices

If your preferred cleaner is booked, Helen never lies or substitutes someone quietly. She explains honestly and offers their next open days or another top-rated cleaner.

4. Real WhatsApp Examples: Before vs After

Here is how conversations actually play out now compared to the old behavior:

Example 1: Customer asks for a cleaner who is booked (Real Message from Prod Logs) Fixed
✕ Before (Old Way: False Promise)
Customer: "Hi, Is Zin available on next Sunday 11 Oct?"
Helen: "Yes! Zin is available on Sunday 11 Oct at 9:00 AM or 2:00 PM. Would you like me to book this for you?"
The Problem: Zin was actually fully booked on 11 Oct. Helen offered open times from other cleaners, leading to surprise and frustration when Zin could not show up.
✓ Now (New Way: Transparent & Helpful)
Customer: "Hi, Is Zin available on next Sunday 11 Oct?"
Helen: "Sunday 11 Oct is not free for Zin. Her next open Sundays are 18 Oct and 1 Nov. Would either of those work, or would you like me to look for another vetted cleaning expert who is free on 11 Oct?"
The Result: 100% truthful. The customer can either pick another date with their favorite cleaner, or choose an available teammate for their urgent weekend clean.
Example 2: Customer asks for a cleaner who covers a different district Fixed
✕ Before (Old Way: Location Ignored)
Customer: "Need a clean with Wyne in Jurong West next Tuesday."
Helen: "Sure! How about Tuesday at 10:00 AM with Wyne?"
The Problem: Wyne's base is in Queenstown/Clementi. She does not travel to Jurong West. Booking this would force a cleaner cancellation or unreasonable travel.
✓ Now (New Way: Respects Travel Areas)
Customer: "Need a clean with Wyne in Jurong West next Tuesday."
Helen: "Wyne focuses on Queenstown and Clementi, so she does not cover Jurong West. I can pair you with one of our top-rated experts who covers Jurong West for Tuesday - would that work?"
The Result: Clear expectations set immediately. The cleaner is protected from impossible commutes, and the customer gets someone who genuinely covers their neighborhood.
Example 3: Regular customer who doesn't specify a cleaner Unaffected
✓ Standard Booking Experience Stays Fast & Smooth
Customer: "Book me a 3-hour clean for next Saturday morning in Toa Payoh."
Helen: "Saturday morning works well! We have slots open at 9:00 AM or 10:00 AM in Toa Payoh. Which time do you prefer?"
Guaranteed: If a customer does not request a specific cleaner, Helen never slows them down or asks unnecessary questions. Regular bookings stay fast and straightforward.
