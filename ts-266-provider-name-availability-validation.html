1. Executive Summary & Root Cause

The Problem

When customers asked whether a specific cleaner was free (e.g., "Is Zin available next Sunday?" or "Can I book with Wyne next week?"), Helen recognized the cleaner name in intent interpretation, but the Step-2 tool execution layer dropped the provider constraint.

Specifically, check_availability and find_next_availability tool definitions did not expose cleaner_name. The underlying RPC get_available_start_times was called with p_provider_id = NULL, returning generic slots across all cleaners. Helen would either hallucinate that the requested cleaner was free at those times (silent substitution) or provide misleading availability.

The Resolution: Exposed cleaner_name on availability tool parameters, integrated findProviderByName() within tools/availability.ts, passed the resolved provider_id into SQL availability RPCs, attached structured provider constraint flags (searched_provider_name, provider_available, provider_unavailable), and enforced provider-specific SOP guidelines in step2-tool-loop.ts and helen-knowledge.ts.
